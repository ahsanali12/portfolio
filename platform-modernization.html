The Challenge

The client's core HR platform was struggling under the weight of a 7-year-old monolithic architecture. As user acquisition tripled, the system became brittle, expensive, and difficult to update.

The Solution: Strategic Pivot

Implementing a domain-driven design (DDD) approach to decouple the system and automate the lifecycle.

Microservices Architecture

Decomposed the monolith into 12 distinct services, each independently deployable and scalable based on real-time traffic demand.

Automated CI/CD

Shifted from monthly manual releases to hourly automated deployments with integrated security scanning and smoke tests.

Event-Driven Integration

Utilized message brokers to ensure high availability and eventual consistency across distributed data stores.
